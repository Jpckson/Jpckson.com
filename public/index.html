osuPedia & mySettings.gg July 2023 - Now  |  Node.JS / Bun, Express.JS, MongoDB, Nunjucks osuPedia, later rebranded to mySettings.gg was my first attempt at web development, and the project that taught me almost everything I know about the field.

The goal was to make a website to showcase pro osu! player's settings since one didn't exist. I started with the Live Server extension and static HTML files with some CSS, no rendering, had no clue what that was.

Eventually another site popped up that was better than mine in every way.

Enrolled in High School and College symultaneously, I slowly pivoted to user generated content, where anyone could add their own settings.

Over the next year I learned how REST APIs worked, started working with Express, switched to Bun, and started using MongoDB to store user data. I slowly pivoted to the site being for all games, not just osu! players, and then I rebranded to mySettings.gg - A link user's could throw in their bio.

I'm still working on the project today, innovating on that original idea.
Rate My Flight May 2026 - Now  |  Bun, Express.JS, MongoDB, Nunjucks RateMyFlight was my second serious attempt at making a website.

I was going through an aviation / travel phase, and during research I noticed a lack of user-generated reviews for airlines and aircraft.

The same few reviewers dominated the space with their long-winded reviews.
So I tried to change that.

I released the site after ~2 weeks of work, but found trouble in marketting my work, resulting in a less than optimal number of reviews, but an important milestone, demonstrating the speed I could take an idea from concept to launch.
Codenames Words June 2026 - Now  |  Astro, Express.JS I build things I wish existed.
I wished a site with word lists for the game Codenames existed.
So I built it!

Originally I wrote this site using Bun and Express.JS in ~5 days, but decided to rewrite it a month later in Astro to get experience with frameworks.

Astro was the perfect fit for this site and a great introduction to frameworks in general and since then, I've started learning React as well.

Learning Astro and rewriting the site took about 5 days and was well worth it.
